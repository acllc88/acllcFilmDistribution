Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Applies to acllc.com and all AC LLC services.

AC LLC ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or use our services, how we use it, and your rights. By using this site or submitting a project, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to do the work:

  • Contact details — name, email address, company or production name, phone number if you provide one.
  • Project information — title, genre, runtime, festival history, budget range, target platforms, screener links, and any materials you send us (synopsis, press kits, key art, masters).
  • Payment information — processed entirely by Stripe or PayPal. We never see or store your full card number, CVV, or PayPal credentials.
  • Site usage — standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages visited, referring URL) and, if you accept, anonymous analytics cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • To review submissions and decide whether to take on a project.
  • To deliver our distribution, QC, metadata, festival and rights services.
  • To send you status updates, royalty statements and invoices.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use your project materials for anything other than the work we agreed to do.

3. Sharing

We share information only where necessary:

  • Streaming platforms and partners — only the metadata, deliverables and rights information needed to place your title, and only with your agreement.
  • Service providers — Stripe and PayPal for payments; our hosting and email providers. They are bound by their own privacy terms.
  • Legal — if required by law, court order or to protect our rights.

4. Cookies

This site uses essential cookies to make it work and, optionally, anonymous analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can disable cookies in your browser; the site will still work, but some features may not.

5. Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to deliver the services and meet legal/tax obligations — typically 7 years for financial records. Project materials for titles we don't take on are deleted within 90 days of a pass decision, unless you ask us to keep them for a future slate.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of marketing emails at any time. Email distribution@acllc.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls, least-privilege access to client materials). No system is 100% secure; if we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we'll notify you promptly.

8. Children

This site and our services are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version. Material changes will be communicated by email to active clients.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: distribution@acllc.com · AC LLC, Los Angeles, California.
