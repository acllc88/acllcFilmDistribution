From deliverables to royalties, we run the full pipeline — so you focus on the next film, not on spec sheets, QC reports and rights paperwork.

The process

From festival laurels to now streaming

A four-step pipeline refined over hundreds of releases. You keep creative control — we handle the machinery.

1

Submit your project

Email us a screener link, synopsis and budget range. Five minutes — no forms, no fees, no obligation.

2

Review & greenlight

We review and tell you exactly which platforms fit your film — and which don't, honestly.

3

We prep everything

Masters, captions, metadata, key art, rights paperwork and platform deliverables — end to end.

4

Go live & earn

Your film premieres across platforms. Launch report on day one, royalty statements quarterly.
